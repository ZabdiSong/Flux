# Source notes and corrections

These notes distinguish ambiguities in the supplied files from the worked solutions added by Flux.

- Lecture page 18 still depicts all four corner charges although its text removes the two bottom charges. The solution follows the written condition.
- Lecture pages 22 and 65 treat the wire as long. The simple infinite-wire result requires distance from the wire to be much smaller than its length and the observation point to be far from the ends. Flux also gives the exact centered finite-wire result.
- Lecture page 66 uses a nonconducting infinite sheet, giving `E = σ/(2ε₀)` on each side. Just outside a conductor surface, the boundary result is `E = σ/ε₀`.
- Lecture page 68 contains a nonuniform-insulating-sphere formula unrelated to its stated hollow-conductor prompt. The Flux worked example solves the conductor problem and flags the pasted formula.
- Lecture pages 36, 56, 71, and 86 contain no additional problem content.
- Quiz 8.6 question 31 prints its field reference at `±z` where the intended boundary reference is `±z₀`. The explanation states that intended reading.
- Quiz 8.6 questions 36–38 label the flux through surface Y as `−4Φ₀`; low-quality extraction can look like `−Φ₄`.
- Quiz 9.2 question 70 gives a two-dimensional field that is not globally conservative. The keyed horizontal-path integral can be evaluated, but it should not be described as a path-independent electrostatic potential.
- Quiz 10.2 questions involving charge shared by separated spheres use the standard textbook approximation that each sphere's potential is `kQ/R` and mutual influence is negligible.
